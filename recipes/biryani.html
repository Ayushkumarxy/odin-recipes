Biryani

Home

Description

Biryani is a flavorful rice dish made with rice, spices, and vegetables or meat.

Ingredients

  • Basmati rice
  • Onion
  • Tomato
  • Ginger-garlic paste
  • Biryani spices
  • Cooking oil

Steps

  1. Wash and soak the basmati rice.
  2. Heat oil and cook the onions until golden.
  3. Add the spices, tomato, and ginger-garlic paste.
  4. Add the rice and required water.
  5. Cook until the rice is tender and the biryani is ready.
