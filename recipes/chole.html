Chole

Description

Chole is a popular Indian dish made with chickpeas, onions, tomatoes, and aromatic spices.

Ingredients

  • Chickpeas
  • Onion
  • Tomato
  • Ginger-garlic paste
  • Chole masala
  • Cooking oil
  • Salt
  • Water

Steps

  1. Soak the chickpeas and cook them until soft.
  2. Heat oil and cook the chopped onions.
  3. Add tomatoes, ginger-garlic paste, and spices.
  4. Add the cooked chickpeas and mix well.
  5. Cook for a few minutes and serve hot.
