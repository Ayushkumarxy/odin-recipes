Pizza

Description

Pizza is a popular dish made with a baked dough base, tomato sauce, cheese, and different toppings.

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Onion
  • Capsicum
  • Oregano
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Prepare the pizza dough.
  2. Spread tomato sauce over the dough.
  3. Add cheese and your preferred toppings.
  4. Sprinkle oregano and other seasonings.
  5. Bake until the crust is cooked and the cheese melts.
